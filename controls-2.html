Case File 04-B · Meridian Regional Health Network

Your First Week on the GRC Bench

You've just started as a junior analyst on Meridian Regional Health Network's Governance, Risk & Compliance team — a three-hospital, twelve-clinic system that stores patient records, runs legacy medical devices on its floor network, and is nine days from a scheduled HIPAA security controls audit.

Your supervisor has handed you a stack of recent security decisions, incident logs, and facilities notices from across the network — some already implemented, some still pending sign-off. Before Friday's audit prep meeting, she needs you to correctly classify each one: which category of control it belongs to (Managerial, Operational, Technical, or Physical), and which functional type it serves (Preventive, Detective, Corrective, Deterrent, Directive, or Compensating).

Get the classification wrong in the audit report, and Meridian risks a finding. Get it right, and you're building the kind of judgment auditors — and hiring managers — actually trust.
