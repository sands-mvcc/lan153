📋 Instructions

  • Evaluate their position in the defense supply chain
  • Assess the sensitivity of information they handle

🎖️ CMMC Levels Overview

  • Level 1 (Foundational): 17 basic controls for Federal Contract Information (FCI)
  • Level 2 (Advanced): 110 NIST SP 800-171 controls for Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
  • Level 3 (Expert): 130 controls (NIST SP 800-171 + 800-172) for highly sensitive CUI
