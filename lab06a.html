COURSE MODULE · CAPTURING NETWORK TRAFFIC

Welcome to the NorthPoint Logistics Security Operations Center

You are the newest analyst on the NorthPoint Logistics SOC team. Your job is to review packet captures pulled from network sensors placed around the company's wired and wireless infrastructure, determine what — if anything — is wrong, and document your findings the way a real analyst would.

Learning Objectives

  • 1Use a simulated packet-capture interface to review live traffic captures and apply Wireshark-style display filters.
  • 2Interpret captured packet data — source/destination addresses, ports, protocol flags, and payload info — to spot anomalies.
  • 3Identify the traffic signatures of six common network-based attacks: network/port scanning, ARP spoofing, brute-force authentication attacks, DNS spoofing, SYN flood denial-of-service, and on-path (man-in-the-middle) credential sniffing.
  • 4Attribute each incident to its likely source and connect evidence to a recommended mitigation, aligned to MITRE ATT&CK and CompTIA Security+ objectives.

The Network You're Watching

NorthPoint Logistics runs a small routed network with a wired workstation/server segment and a separate wireless VLAN for laptops and mobile devices. Sensors mirror traffic from four points into your SOC console.

Network device SOC sensor / mirror port

How This Shift Works

You'll work six tickets, one at a time. Each ticket includes an alert, a captured packet log you can filter and inspect, and a short analysis form. Use hints if you get stuck — they cost a few points, but they never block your progress. You can always reveal the answer and move on if you're stuck.
