💾 Task 2: Asset Identification

Catalog and classify FreshFlow Systems' critical assets

🎯 Your Mission

Identify and categorize all critical assets that FreshFlow Systems must protect. Focus on data assets, physical assets, and intellectual property that would impact business operations if compromised.

📋 Student Guidance

Assets are anything of value to the organization. Consider data, systems, facilities, equipment, and people. Think about what would cause the most damage if lost, stolen, or compromised.

Asset Categories to Address:

  • Data Assets: Customer data, financial records, intellectual property
  • Physical Assets: Facilities, equipment, infrastructure
  • Technology Assets: Servers, networks, applications, databases
  • Human Assets: Employees, contractors, partners
  • Intangible Assets: Reputation, brand, trade secrets
Asset Classification Example:
Asset: Customer Payment Database
Type: Data Asset
Criticality: High
Impact if Compromised: Financial loss, regulatory fines, reputation damage
Protection Requirements: Encryption, access controls, backup, monitoring
⚠️ Important: Consider both obvious assets (like servers) and less obvious ones (like employee knowledge and company reputation). All can be targets for cyber attacks.

  • Monitoring & Response: SIEM, logging, incident response tools
  • Physical Security: Access controls, surveillance, environmental
