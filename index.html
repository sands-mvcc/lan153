🎓 Course Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

  1. 1Understand computer, network, and information security and why it is important.
  2. 2Define and explain the concepts of network confidentiality, integrity, availability, and non-repudiation.
  3. 3Demonstrate the management’s role in the development, implementation, and maintenance of the network security.
  4. 4Understand the value of education, training, and awareness programs to the organization.
  5. 5Define risk management and its role in security policy and security architecture.
  6. 6Understand the relationship between risk, threats, vulnerabilities, and countermeasures.
  7. 7Identify and describe risk mitigation strategies.
  8. 8Define and describe disaster recovery plans and an incident response plan.
  9. 9Define and describe a business continuity plan.
  10. 10Describe the various functions related to database security.
  11. 11Describe and understand a feasibility study.
  12. 12Understand the difference between policies, procedures, standards, and guidelines.
  13. 13Understand the process of encryption and define the key cryptography terms.
  14. 14Understand the difference between asymmetric and symmetric encryption.
  15. 15Discuss scanning and analysis tools.
  16. 16Describe the various types of firewalls and the various types of intrusion detection systems.
  17. 17Describe the operation of Virtual Private Networks.
  18. 18Understand the importance of authentication and the characteristics of a good password.
  19. 19Describe the various approaches to biometrics access control.
  20. 20Understand the position of the network security element within the organization.
  21. 21Understand the relationship between an organization's employment practices and policies and the network security function.
  22. 22Understand the position of the CIRT element within the security function.
  23. 23Explain the various credentials that can be acquired by the security professional and their value.
  24. 24Identify and explain the major laws relating to network security.
  25. 25Understand the issue of privacy and its impact upon the network security function.
📚 Course Lessons — 12 Days
Day 1 General Concepts
Security Fundamentals
CIA triad, AAA, security control types, zero trust, and an orientation to the Security+ exam structure and five domains.
Key terms: CIA triad, AAA, Zero Trust, Non-repudiation →
Day 2 General Concepts
Cryptography & PKI
Symmetric vs. asymmetric encryption, hashing, digital signatures, certificates, PKI lifecycle, and TLS certificate analysis.
Key terms: PKI, Digital Signature, CA, Obfuscation →
Day 3 Threats & Vulns
Threat Actors & Attack Surface
Threat actor types, motivations, attack vectors, social engineering tactics, malware categories, and password attacks.
Key terms: Threat Actor, Phishing, Ransomware, Supply Chain →
Day 4 Threats & Vulns
Vulnerabilities & Mitigation
OS, app, mobile, cloud, and web vulnerabilities; patching, hardening, segmentation, isolation, and access control as mitigations.
Key terms: Hardening, Segmentation, Misconfiguration, Exploit →
Day 5 Architecture
Secure Architecture
Secure design across on-premises, cloud, virtualization, IoT, ICS, and infrastructure as code. Data classification and resilience.
Key terms: IaC, IoT, ICS, Redundancy, High Availability →
Day 6 Architecture
Network & Infrastructure Security
Firewalls, IDS/IPS, DNS filtering, NAC, DLP, and endpoint security. Matching controls to threats and enterprise defense strategy.
Key terms: Firewall, IDS/IPS, NAC, DLP, Endpoint Security →
Day 7 Security Ops
Identity & Access Management
Authentication, authorization, accounting, SSO, MFA, privileged access, provisioning/deprovisioning, and access control models.
Key terms: MFA, SSO, Least Privilege, RBAC, PAM →
Day 8 Security Ops
Security Operations & Monitoring
Secure baselines, logging, monitoring tools, asset management, wireless security, sandboxing, and operational security workflows.
Key terms: Baseline, Asset Inventory, Sandboxing, Alerting →
Day 9 Security Ops
Vulnerability Management & Incident Response
Vulnerability scanning, analysis, remediation, validation, incident response phases, root cause analysis, and digital forensics.
Key terms: Vuln Scan, Remediation, IR, Forensic Evidence →
Day 10 Program Mgmt
Security Governance & Policy
Policies, standards, procedures, governance roles, compliance obligations, privacy frameworks, and security awareness training.
Key terms: Policy, Standard, Governance, Compliance, Privacy →
Day 11 Program Mgmt
Risk, Third-Party & Assessment
Risk identification, assessment, appetite and tolerance, risk register, BIA, third-party risk management, audits, and pen testing.
Key terms: Risk Register, BIA, Vendor Risk, Audit, Attestation →
Day 12 All Domains
Final Review & Exam Readiness
Full-domain review, exam traps, performance-based question workshop, pacing strategy, and cumulative practice assessment.
Key terms: PBQ, Domain Weight, Distractor, Exam Pacing →
🧩 Learning Activities — 10 Activities
Activity 1 Learning Activity
Cybersecurity Principles and Controls
Apply the CIA triad, non-repudiation, and defense in depth to classify security controls and match them to the risks they address.
Covers: CIA · Control Types · Defense in Depth →
Activity 2 Learning Activity
Cryptography
Explore the encryption process and key terminology, and compare symmetric and asymmetric encryption in practical scenarios.
Covers: Encryption · Keys · Symmetric · Asymmetric →
Activity 3 Learning Activity
Hashing
Generate and compare hash values to verify data integrity, and see why small changes to input produce completely different outputs.
Covers: Hash Functions · Integrity · Collisions →
Activity 4 Learning Activity
Cyber Security Frameworks (NIST 171)
Examine NIST SP 800-171 requirements for protecting controlled unclassified information and map them to organizational practices.
Covers: NIST 800-171 · CUI · Control Families →
Activity 5 Learning Activity
Multifactor Authentication
Evaluate authentication factors, the characteristics of a strong password, multifactor authentication, and biometric approaches.
Covers: Factors · MFA · Passwords · Biometrics →
Activity 6 Learning Activity
Identity Management and Access Control
Work through the identity lifecycle and compare access control models, applying least privilege to provisioning and deprovisioning decisions.
Covers: Identity Lifecycle · SSO · RBAC · Least Privilege →
Activity 7 Learning Activity
Access Control Matrix
Build and interpret an access control matrix that defines which subjects may perform which actions on which objects.
Covers: Subjects · Objects · Permissions · ACLs →
Activity 8 Learning Activity
Vulnerability Scanning and SIEMs
Interpret vulnerability scan results and SIEM alerts, correlate log events, and prioritize findings for remediation.
Covers: Scanning · Log Correlation · Alert Triage →
Activity 9 Learning Activity
Incident Response
Respond to a simulated security incident, applying the incident response phases and the role of the CIRT within the security function.
Covers: IR Phases · CIRT · Evidence · Recovery →
Activity 10 Learning Activity
Risk Management
Analyze the relationship between risk, threats, vulnerabilities, and countermeasures, then select appropriate risk mitigation strategies.
Covers: Risk Analysis · Countermeasures · Mitigation →
🔬 Lab Activities — 8 Labs
Lab 1 Hands-On Lab
Malware Analysis
Examine suspicious files using static and dynamic analysis techniques to identify behavior and indicators of compromise.
Covers: Static Analysis · Dynamic Analysis · IOCs →
Lab 2 Hands-On Lab
ARP Attack
Perform and observe an ARP poisoning attack, then apply detection and mitigation techniques to protect the local network.
Covers: ARP Spoofing · Man-in-the-Middle · Mitigation →
Lab 3 Hands-On Lab
Cypher Ledger
Apply cipher techniques to encode, decode, and verify protected records in a hands-on ledger exercise.
Covers: Ciphers · Encoding · Verification →
Lab 4 Hands-On Lab
Configuring a Network Firewall
Design and configure firewall rules that enforce a security policy, then test that permitted and blocked traffic behave as intended.
Covers: Rule Sets · Default Deny · Testing →
Lab 5 Hands-On Lab
Pentesting
Follow the penetration testing process from reconnaissance through exploitation and reporting within defined rules of engagement.
Covers: Recon · Exploitation · Reporting →
Lab 6 Hands-On Lab
Packet Capturing and Analysis
Capture live network traffic, apply filters, and analyze protocol behavior to spot suspicious or unencrypted communications.
Covers: Capture · Filters · Protocol Analysis →
Lab 7 Hands-On Lab
Intrusion Detection Systems
Deploy an intrusion detection system, generate test attacks, and review the alerts it produces to evaluate detection effectiveness.
Covers: Signatures · Alerts · Tuning →
Lab 8 Hands-On Lab
Configuring VPNs
Configure and verify a virtual private network tunnel to protect data in transit between remote users or sites.
Covers: Tunneling · Encryption · Remote Access →
