You are a junior analyst in the MVCC cyber lab. Security operations wants a capture of the lab network: confirm what normal traffic looks like, find anything that isn’t normal, and write it up.

Work through the three steps in order. Build the knowledge here, run the capture and the ten analysis missions in step 2, then save or print your report in step 3.

The lab switch mirrors (SPAN) the whole lab VLAN to your workstation’s port. With promiscuous mode on, your network card accepts frames meant for other hosts, so you can see their conversations too. Other devices may also be on the network.

Capture filters versus display filters

Capture filters (BPF) decide what is saved; anything they exclude is gone. Display filters only hide packets you already captured.
