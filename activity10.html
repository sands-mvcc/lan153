    4. Qualitative analysis

    Clinic-wide risks involve patient trust, regulators, and safety, which are hard to price. Rate them on the clinic’s scales and plot them on the heat map.

    K3 MethodsS3 Risk matrixA1 Prioritize
    Lakeshore’s rating scales

    Probability

    1. Rare: less than once in 20 years
    2. Unlikely: once in 5 to 20 years
    3. Possible: once in 1 to 5 years
    4. Probable: 1 to 3 times a year
    5. Highly probable: more than 3 times a year

    Impact

    1. Very low: no disruption, no data exposed
    2. Minor: internal disruption under 4 hours, no patients affected
    3. Moderate: patient services delayed up to 1 day, or fewer than 100 records exposed
    4. Major: services down 1 to 7 days, or 100 to 10,000 records exposed with regulatory reporting
    5. Severe: services down more than a week, or more than 10,000 records exposed
